The short version

  • No account and no Aurora Radar server. There is nothing to sign up for, and we never receive your places or your location.
  • Your places stay on your phone. To get a cloud forecast, the app sends MET Norway your place's location rounded to about 1 km.
  • Location only when you ask, approximate only, and never in the background.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are optional and can be switched off at any time in Settings › Privacy.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, with Google's consent form where the law requires one. Aurora Radar Pro removes them, and Pro users' phones never request ads.

1. Who we are

Aurora Radar (com.pkay.auroraradar, "the app") is developed and published by Levon Momchyan, an independent developer based in Armenia ("we", "us"). For the limited personal data described here, we are the data controller. Contact details are in section 18.

2. What stays on your phone

  • Saved places: the name and coordinates of the towns you choose, and their time zones.
  • Settings: alert level, quiet hours, theme, units and your privacy choices.
  • Your approximate position, only if you tap "Use my approximate location". The app asks Android for approximate (coarse) location at that moment, labels the position with the nearest town's name, and saves it as one of your places.
  • Working data: the cached forecast, alert history, ad-frequency counters and your Pro status, so the app works offline and alerts are not repeated.

We cannot see any of this. It is deleted when you remove a place, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

3. What we never collect

  • Precise (GPS) location, or any location in the background.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or files.
  • Payment details: Google Play handles payments, and we never see your card.
  • Coordinates or place names in analytics. The app blocks any analytics event that would contain them.

5. Usage statistics and crash reports

Both are off until you make a choice during setup. The two switches start off if your phone's language, SIM or network region is in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, and on elsewhere; either way you see them and decide. You can change them any time in Settings › Privacy.

Usage statistics never include coordinates, place names or your advertising ID, and they are never used for advertising: the app tells Firebase that advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation are always denied for analytics.

6. Advertising

The free version shows muted ads from Google AdMob, rated PG or lower. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices. Elsewhere, Google may personalise ads using your advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.

Aurora Radar Pro removes all ads; Pro users' phones don't request ads at all. Learn how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

7. Purchases and reviews

Aurora Radar Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy; the app receives only the purchase status and an order token, checks the purchase signature on your phone, and stores your Pro status locally.

The app may occasionally offer Google Play's in-app review prompt. Google runs that flow; we only see the public review you choose to post.

8. Permissions

  • Approximate location (asks first): only when you tap "Use my approximate location".
  • Notifications (asks first): to deliver aurora alerts.
  • Internet and network state: to download forecasts and to wait for a connection before background checks.
  • Run at startup: so alert checks continue after your phone restarts.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: used by Android's WorkManager to finish a short alert check, mainly on Android 11 and older.
  • Google Play Billing: to sell and restore Pro.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services (Privacy Sandbox): used by Google's ad library in the free version.
  • Play Install Referrer: lets Google Analytics for Firebase see which Play Store link led to the install, only if usage statistics are on.

9. Notifications, widget and sharing

Alerts are worked out on your phone from public forecast data and shown as local notifications. No push service and no server of ours is involved. The home-screen widget reads the same on-device data.

"Share tonight's forecast" creates an image on your phone (with the place name and chance) and opens Android's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you pick an app to share with.

10. Android backup

If Android backup is on, your saved places, alert history and settings are included in your device's Google backup, so they can move to a new phone. On Android 12 and later the app joins cloud backup only when it is end-to-end encrypted. Ad counters, rewarded unlocks, the purchase cache and cached forecasts are never backed up. We cannot read your backup; you control it in Android settings.

11. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete a place, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Google Analytics event data is kept for 2 months. Crashlytics crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • NOAA and MET Norway handle their request logs under their own policies: NOAA/NWS privacy and MET Norway privacy.

12. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Because we run no server, there is no database of users that could be breached.

13. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Forecast requests to NOAA and MET Norway: needed to provide the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Usage statistics, crash reports and personalised ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw in Settings at any time.
  • Non-personalised ads, Remote Config and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding and running a free app (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Purchases: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

14. International transfers

Google may process data in the United States and other countries under its standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. NOAA is in the United States; MET Norway is in Norway (EEA).

15. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. Because the app has no account, we hold no profile about you. You can:

  • turn off usage statistics, crash reports or ad personalisation in Settings › Privacy (ad personalisation via "Ad privacy choices" where it is offered, or your Android ad settings);
  • delete everything the app stores by clearing its data or uninstalling it;
  • ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app-instance ID by emailing us.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

16. Children

Aurora Radar is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

17. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
