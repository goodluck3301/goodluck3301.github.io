1. Shotday is not medical advice

Shotday is a personal record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, or recommend a dose.

  • The estimated medication level comes from a simple model based on published half-life data. It is an approximation, not a lab value; real levels vary from person to person.
  • The dose calculator does arithmetic on the numbers you enter. It can't check whether those numbers are right. Always follow your prescriber's or pharmacy's instructions, and double-check every dose before you inject.
  • Reminders can fail or arrive late, for example if notifications are blocked, battery saving delays them, or the phone is off. Don't rely on Shotday as your only reminder for a medication.
  • Weight trends, insights and reports describe your own entries. They are not a medical assessment.

Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your treatment. Never change or stop a medication because of something the app shows. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
